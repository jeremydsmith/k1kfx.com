By hobby

On the air

Radio is where physics, engineering and computers meet. I love teaching kids the science behind the magic, whether it's GMRS, building a Moxon antenna, fox hunting or tracking satellites. The teaching is still in my blood.

Parks on the Air

I set up a portable station in a park and make as many contacts as I can, often at parks that few people have activated. Afterwards we color in a map of every state we reached.
